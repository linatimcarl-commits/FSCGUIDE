Food Safety

1.1 Available potable water supply (Critical) ▶
Requirement:
- Schedule analysis is once every 6 months.
- Follow government requirements if more frequent testing is mandated.
- Required sampling points: filtered back sink and/or counter faucet (if available), and ice from ice machine, outsourced, or borrowed.
- Outsourced water and ice must have a certificate of potability.
- Hetorotrophic Plate Count / Total Plate Count / Total Bacterial Count should be < 500CFU/ml.

1.2 All foods items shall be free from spoilage, including but not limited to mold, off-odors, discoloration and textural degradation (Critical) ▶
Requirement:
• No spoiled item is served to the customers or found in any part of the store.
• No SD8 (molds) on foods
• Off-odor or off-flavor due to fementation is considered spoiled.
• Conduct Sensory Test; tomtatoes, sliced pineapple, soupbase, vanmix, rice, gravy, sliced egg, noodles, etc.

1.3 Meet standard internal temperature of cold products (Critical) ▶
Requirement:
• Cold products meet standard temperature control of < 40°F
• Applies to raw TCS foods (e.g. meat, sauces, poultry, fish, dairy)
• High risk >60°F / 15°C
• Medium Risk 40.1–59.9°F / 4.1–14.9°C
• Low risk <40°F / 4°C

1.4 Meet standard internal temperature of cooked products. NO RAW MEAT IS SERVED TO CUSTOMER(Critical) ▶
Requirement:
• Foods that require time/temperature control for safety are cooked to proper internal temperatures.
• NO RAW BLOOD AND/OR TRANSLUSCENT MEAT IS SERVED TO CUSTOMER.

1.5 Meet standard internal temperature of hot-held products.(Critical) ▶
Requirement:
• Hot foods that require time/temperature control for safety are maintained at 150 Deegres F (60 Deegres C)
• HIGH RISK <135 DEEGRES F / 57 DEEGRES C
- MEDIUM RISK 135.1 - 139.9 DEEGRES F/57.1-59.9 DEEGRES C.
- LOW RISK > 140 DEEGRES F / 60 DEEGRES C.

1.6 Product withing shelf-life(Critical) ▶
Requirement:
• All food items within primary shelf-life. any part of the store.
• Products beyond shelf life are segregated, properly labeled and/or disposed accordingly.
• It covers processed vegetables as well.
• Shelf-life extension is supported by R&D memo.

1.7 Absence of critical SD on food. (Critical) ▶
Requirement:
• No critical SD in served food, food held at the station or staging equipment .
• ANY PEST INSIDE THE WTER STORAGE unit is considered a contaminant in food.
• All others SDs taht are not listed under critical SDs but are life threatening or big enough in size (>7mm) to cause injury will fall under SD!*.
- Outsourced water and ice must have a certificate of potability.

1.8 Fit-to-work food handlers (Critical) ▶
Requirement:
• Personnel must report illnesses to the manager before they come to work.
• There should be no personnel working around food or food contact surfaces if with any of the following: ➢ Persistent Sneezing
➢ Coughing
➢ Runny nose that causes discharges from the eyes, nose, or mouth
➢ Uncovered Infected/bleeding wounds, skin infections, or sores
• Persons displaying symptoms such as vomiting, diarrhea, jaundice, or sore throat
• With fever (≥ 37.5C ) are excluded from the establishment

Requirement:
• Hands that may have become contaminated are washed using hot water and soap for 20 seconds and dried using disposable towels or a heated-air handdrying device
• Hot Water (100°F/38°C in 60 seconds after opening the faucet)
• All Handwashing facilities at Kitchen area are accessible

4. Sanitize in the third sink by submerging the items in a chemical
sanitizing solution for at least one minute 5. Air dry all items
• For stores with dishwashing machine, proper loading and precleaning SOPs must be observed
• If the dishwashing machine is not available or functional, use disposable food packaging (if manual washing SOPs are not present)
• Dishwashing machine is properly functioning and at the right temperature.
• Food-contact surfaces/ T&Us are properly cleaned and sanitized (at least every 4 hours)
